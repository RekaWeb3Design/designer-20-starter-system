# Validation Question Generator

## Process
1. Read personas.md and journeys.md. Collect every [ASSUMPTION] — these
   are exactly what validation must test.
2. Qualitative interview guide: 8-12 open questions, ordered from warm-up
   to core assumptions. No leading questions.
3. Quantitative survey: 6-10 closed questions (scales, choices) that can
   be answered in under 3 minutes.
4. For every question, note WHICH assumption or journey stage it validates.

## Quality bar
- Every [ASSUMPTION] from the earlier outputs is covered by at least
  one question.
- Interview questions ask about past behaviour, not hypotheticals
  ("mikor legutóbb...", not "használnád-e...").
